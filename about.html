About AgentsCalc

What AgentsCalc is

AgentsCalc is a free collection of property & casualty insurance calculators and plain-English guides, built by Alpha2AI.

It serves two groups: insurance professionals — agents, brokers, CSRs and underwriters — who need quick, checkable math for premiums, cancellations, commissions and ratios, and people shopping for home, auto or renters insurance who want to understand their coverage before they buy.

How the calculators are built

Every calculator shows its formula, so you can check the result yourself rather than trust a black box. Each one comes with step-by-step instructions, a worked example and answers to common questions.

The worked example published on every calculator page is checked by an automated test suite that runs before each update goes live. If a change to the math would alter a published example, the update is stopped until the example and the math agree again.

The formulas are standard industry methods — pro-rata and short-rate earned premium, coinsurance, loss ratios, experience rating and similar. Where practice varies by insurer or state, the page says so.

What AgentsCalc is not

AgentsCalc is not an insurance company or an insurance agency. It doesn’t sell insurance, recommend specific insurers, or give quotes.

Results are estimates for education and discussion. Your policy wording, your insurer’s rules and the law in your state decide what is actually covered and what you pay. For decisions about your own coverage, talk to a licensed agent or your insurer.

Free, with no sign-up

Every calculator and guide is free to use without an account. The calculations run in your browser, so the numbers you enter stay on your device.
