Total Insured Value (TIV) Calculator

Build a schedule of values and total insured value across locations: building, contents and business income.

How it works

Total insured value is the sum of building, business personal property and business income values across all locations. It drives property pricing, catastrophe modeling and reinsurance.

Formula

TIV = Σ (Building + Contents + Business income) across locations.

How to use it

  1. List each location.
  2. Enter building, contents and BI values.
  3. Review totals and concentration.

Worked example

Three locations with $6.7M building, $2.97M contents and $2.85M BI have a TIV of $12.52M; the main plant is 67% of it.

Frequently asked questions

Why does concentration matter?

A high share of TIV at one location means one event can produce a very large loss — underwriters price and limit for that.

Is BI always included in TIV?

Usually, yes, for commercial property schedules, though some carriers report it separately.
