Check your coverage before you buy

Free calculators for homeowners, renters and drivers. Work out how much coverage you need and what your deductibles really cost, then compare quotes with confidence.

Home, auto & renters calculators

  • Insurance Coverage Checkup — Answer 13 quick questions about your home, car and household and get a printable checklist of coverage gaps to ask your agent about.
  • Insurance Quote Comparison Tool — Compare home, renters or auto insurance quotes side by side and see what a cheaper quote leaves out: lower liability, less coverage, higher deductibles or actual cash value.
  • Hurricane & Wind Deductible Calculator — Turn a percentage hurricane or wind deductible (1%, 2%, 5%) into dollars, and see what you would pay on a storm claim.
  • Should I Drop Full Coverage? Calculator — Compare what you pay each year for collision and comprehensive with the most you could ever collect, and check the 10% rule.
  • Should I File a Home Insurance Claim? Calculator — Weigh what a small claim would pay against the premium increase that may follow, and find the damage amount where filing pays off.
  • Home Insurance Coverage Calculator — Estimate how much dwelling coverage your home needs from its size and local rebuilding cost, plus typical other-structures, belongings and loss-of-use amounts.
  • Renters Insurance Calculator — Add up your belongings and work out how much personal property, loss-of-use and liability coverage to buy in a renters policy.
  • Umbrella Insurance Calculator — Compare what you could lose in a lawsuit with the liability limit on your home and auto policies, and size an umbrella policy to cover the gap.
  • Gap Insurance Calculator — See how much more you would owe on a car loan than the car is worth, month by month, and when gap insurance stops being needed.
  • Liability Limits Checker — Compare your auto liability limits (like 50/100/25) with what you could lose in a lawsuit, and see suggested limits.

Also useful when you shop

  • Deductible Comparison Calculator — Is a $1,000 deductible worth the savings over $500? Compare the premium you save with the extra you would pay per claim.
  • ACV Depreciation Calculator — See what an older roof, TV or car would actually be paid out at under actual cash value.
  • Pay in Full vs Installments Calculator — Find out what paying your premium monthly really costs compared with paying in full.
  • Pro-Rata Cancellation Calculator — Switching insurers mid-term? Estimate the refund on the policy you cancel.
  • Renewal Increase Calculator — Work out exactly how much your renewal went up, and whether it is time to shop around.
