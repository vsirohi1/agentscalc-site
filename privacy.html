Privacy Policy

Last updated November 9, 2026

The short version

The numbers you type into AgentsCalc stay in your browser. We don’t ask you to create an account, we don’t sell or share personal information, and we don’t use advertising or tracking cookies.

Calculator inputs

Every calculation runs on your own device. Your inputs are not sent to us or stored by us.

If you use “Copy link”, your inputs are written into the link itself so whoever opens it sees the same numbers. Anyone you share that link with can see those inputs, so share it only with people you choose. PDF downloads are created on your device and are not uploaded anywhere.

Visitor statistics

We use Cloudflare Web Analytics to count visits and see which pages are popular. It does not use cookies and does not track you across other websites. It records information such as the page viewed, the referring site, your browser and device type, and your approximate country.

Hosting

The site is hosted on GitHub Pages and uses Cloudflare for its domain name. Like most web hosts, these providers may keep server logs, including IP addresses, for security and to keep the service running. Their own privacy policies apply to that information.

Emails you send us

If you email us through the “Send feedback” link, we receive your email address and message. We use them only to read and reply to your feedback, and we don’t add you to any mailing list.

Children

AgentsCalc is intended for adults and does not knowingly collect information from children.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the date at the top.

Contact

Questions about privacy? Use the “Send feedback” link at the bottom of any page.
