Increased limits factor (ILF)

A multiplier that converts a basic-limits liability premium into the premium for a higher limit.

Because a loss is less likely to reach higher layers, each extra dollar of limit costs less than the one before. An ILF of 1.85 at $1 million means that limit costs 85% more than the basic limit.
