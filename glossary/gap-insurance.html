Gap insurance

Coverage that pays the difference between a totaled car’s value and the loan or lease balance.

When a car is totaled, insurance pays its actual cash value. If you owe more than that, gap insurance pays the rest. Some gap policies don’t cover your deductible. Leases often include it.

Related terms

  • Total loss
  • Actual cash value (ACV)
