Premium

The amount a policyholder pays for insurance coverage for a policy period.

Premium generally reflects the exposure, the rate for the class, rating adjustments such as experience and schedule rating, and fees and taxes.

Related terms

  • Rate
  • Written premium
  • Earned premium
