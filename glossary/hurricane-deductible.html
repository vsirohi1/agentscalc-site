Hurricane deductible

A separate homeowners deductible for hurricane damage, usually a percentage of the dwelling limit.

A 2% hurricane deductible on a $400,000 dwelling limit is $8,000. What triggers it — a named storm, a hurricane watch or warning — is defined in the policy and by state rules.
