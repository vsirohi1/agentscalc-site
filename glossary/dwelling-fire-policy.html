Dwelling fire policy (DP-1, DP-2, DP-3)

A property policy for homes the owner doesn’t live in, most often used to insure rental properties.

DP-1 covers a short list of named perils, DP-2 a broader list, and DP-3 covers the building on an open perils basis. Landlord policies usually add liability and fair rental value coverage.

Related terms

  • Fair rental value
  • Named perils
  • All-risk (open perils)
