Debris removal

Coverage for the cost of clearing away the remains of damaged property after a covered loss.

Many policies include debris removal within the property limit, plus a small additional amount. After a major fire or storm, clearing the site can cost a meaningful share of the rebuild, so it is worth allowing for it in the limit.

Related terms

  • Coverage A (dwelling)
