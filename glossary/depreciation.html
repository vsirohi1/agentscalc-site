Depreciation

The loss in value of property from age, wear and obsolescence, which insurers subtract to arrive at actual cash value.

Adjusters usually depreciate an item based on its age and expected useful life. On a replacement cost policy, depreciation held back from the first payment is “recoverable” once you repair or replace the item.

Related terms

  • Actual cash value (ACV)
  • Replacement cost
