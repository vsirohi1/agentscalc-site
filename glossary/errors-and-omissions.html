Errors and omissions (E&O) insurance

Professional liability coverage for mistakes, oversights or failure to perform professional services.

Insurance agents, real estate agents, consultants and many other professionals carry E&O to cover claims that their advice or work caused a client financial loss. It is usually written on a claims-made basis.
