Business owners policy (BOP)

A package policy for small businesses that combines property and general liability coverage, usually with business income included.

Insurers design BOPs for eligible small and mid-sized businesses with typical risks — offices, shops, restaurants. Bundling the coverages usually costs less than buying them separately.

Workers compensation, commercial auto and professional liability are not part of a BOP and are bought separately.

  • Business interruption (business income)
