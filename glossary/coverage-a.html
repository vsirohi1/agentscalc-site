Coverage A (dwelling)

The part of a homeowners policy that pays to repair or rebuild the house itself.

Coverage A should equal the cost to rebuild the home at current construction prices — not its market value or mortgage balance. Several other coverages are set as percentages of it.

On a condo policy, Coverage A covers the walls-in portion of the unit you are responsible for.
