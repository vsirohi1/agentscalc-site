Captive insurance company

An insurer owned by the businesses it insures, set up to fund their own risks.

A large company or a group of similar businesses forms a captive to insure its own losses, keeping underwriting profit and investment income that would otherwise go to a commercial insurer.

Captives usually buy reinsurance for large losses and work best for owners with predictable losses and strong risk management.
