Additional living expenses (ALE)

Coverage for the extra cost of living elsewhere — hotel, meals, longer commute — while your home is repaired after a covered loss.

ALE pays the increase over your normal living costs, not your whole budget. On a homeowners policy it is part of Coverage D, loss of use, and often has a dollar limit or time limit.

Renters and condo policies include it too, which is one of the less obvious reasons to carry them.
