Business interruption (business income)

Coverage for the income a business loses, and the expenses that continue, while it is closed or slowed by covered property damage.

It pays net income plus continuing expenses such as payroll and rent during the period of restoration, the realistic time to repair and reopen. Extra expense coverage pays the cost of staying open, like a temporary location.

Limits are often set too low; estimating the restoration period honestly is the key step.
