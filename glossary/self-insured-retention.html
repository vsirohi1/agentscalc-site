Self-insured retention (SIR)

An amount the insured pays for each claim — including handling it — before the insurer’s coverage begins.

Unlike a deductible, which the insurer pays first and recovers, an SIR is handled and paid by the insured. Umbrella policies may apply one to claims the underlying policies don’t cover.
