Claims-made policy

A liability policy that covers claims first made against you during the policy period, for incidents after its retroactive date.

Professional liability and many management liability policies are written this way. Coverage depends on when the claim is made, not when the incident happened.

If you cancel or don’t renew, an extended reporting period (“tail”) lets you report later claims for past work.

Related terms

  • Occurrence policy
  • Errors and omissions (E&O) insurance
