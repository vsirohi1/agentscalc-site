Deductible

The part of a covered loss you pay before insurance pays the rest.

A higher deductible lowers the premium but means paying more out of pocket on each claim. Most home and auto deductibles are flat amounts; hurricane, wind and earthquake deductibles are often a percentage of the coverage limit.

Related terms

  • Percentage deductible
  • Self-insured retention (SIR)
