Coverage E (personal liability)

Homeowners, condo and renters coverage for injuries or property damage you cause to others, and the legal costs of defending you.

It applies at home and, for many situations, away from home — a guest slipping on your stairs, your dog biting a neighbor. Common limits are $100,000 to $500,000.

An umbrella policy adds more on top of it.
