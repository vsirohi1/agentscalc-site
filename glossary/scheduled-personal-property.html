Scheduled personal property

Specific valuable items — jewelry, art, collectibles — listed on a policy with their own coverage amounts.

Home and renters policies often cap theft of jewelry and similar items at low amounts. Scheduling them covers each item for its appraised value, often with broader coverage and no deductible.
