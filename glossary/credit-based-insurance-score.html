Credit-based insurance score

A score built from your credit report that many insurers use, where allowed, to help price home and auto insurance.

It is not the same as a lender’s credit score, though it uses similar information — payment history, balances, length of credit history. Rules vary by state; some states don’t allow it in pricing auto insurance.

If an insurer charges more because of your credit report, it must tell you and say which credit bureau provided it.
