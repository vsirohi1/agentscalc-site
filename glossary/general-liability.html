General liability insurance

Business coverage for injuries and property damage to others caused by the business’s premises, operations or products.

It also typically covers personal and advertising injury, such as libel or copyright infringement in advertising. It is usually rated on sales or payroll and written with per-occurrence and aggregate limits.
