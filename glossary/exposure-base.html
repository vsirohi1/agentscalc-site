Exposure base

The unit an insurer uses to measure the size of a risk for rating, such as payroll, sales, vehicles or square feet.

Workers comp is rated per $100 of payroll, many general liability classes per $1,000 of sales, commercial auto per vehicle. Premium generally equals exposure ÷ unit × rate.
