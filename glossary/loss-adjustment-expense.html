Loss adjustment expense (LAE)

The cost of investigating, defending and settling claims, beyond the claim payments themselves.

Allocated LAE can be tied to specific claims, such as defense lawyers; unallocated LAE covers general claims department costs. Loss and LAE ratios include both.
