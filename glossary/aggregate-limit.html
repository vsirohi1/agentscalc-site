Aggregate limit

The most a policy will pay for all covered claims during the policy period, no matter how many claims there are.

A general liability policy might carry $1 million per occurrence and a $2 million aggregate: no single occurrence pays more than $1 million, and the year’s claims together can’t exceed $2 million.

Once the aggregate is used up, the policy pays nothing more until it renews, which is one reason businesses buy umbrella or excess coverage.
