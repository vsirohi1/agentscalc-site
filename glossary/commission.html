Commission

The share of the premium an insurer pays the agent or broker who placed the policy.

Commission is usually a percentage of premium and varies by line and insurer — new business and renewals may pay different rates. Agencies may also earn contingent commissions based on the profitability or growth of their book.

Within an agency, producers are typically paid a split of the commission the agency receives.
