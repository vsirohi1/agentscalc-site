Contingent commission

A bonus an insurer pays an agency based on the profitability, growth or retention of the business it placed.

Also called profit-sharing or bonus commission. A typical agreement pays a percentage of premium when the agency’s loss ratio is below a threshold and its premium volume is above a minimum.

Because it depends on results, it is paid after the year ends and can vary a lot from year to year.
