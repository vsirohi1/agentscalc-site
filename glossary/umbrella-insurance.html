Umbrella insurance

Extra liability coverage that pays above your home and auto limits, and for some claims they don’t cover.

Personal umbrellas are usually sold in $1 million steps and require minimum liability limits on the underlying policies. Businesses buy commercial umbrella or excess policies above their liability coverage.

Related terms

  • Split limits
  • Aggregate limit
