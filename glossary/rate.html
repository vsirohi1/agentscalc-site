Rate

The price per unit of exposure, such as per $100 of payroll or per $1,000 of sales.

Premium is the rate times the number of exposure units, adjusted for rating factors. Comparing rates rather than premiums separates price changes from growth.

Related terms

  • Exposure base
  • Premium
  • Indicated rate

Calculators that use this

  • Rate Per Unit Calculator — Convert a premium into a rate per $100, per $1,000 or per unit of exposure and compare two quotes on the same basis.
  • Renewal Increase Calculator — Calculate the percentage renewal increase and split it into exposure growth and pure rate change.
  • Commercial Premium Calculator — Estimate a commercial insurance premium from exposure, rate, schedule rating, experience mod, minimum premium, fees and taxes.
