All-risk (open perils)

Coverage for any cause of loss that the policy doesn’t specifically exclude.

Instead of listing what is covered, an open perils policy covers everything and then lists exclusions — commonly flood, earthquake, wear and tear, and intentional damage.

A standard homeowners policy (HO-3) covers the house on an open perils basis and belongings on a named perils basis.
