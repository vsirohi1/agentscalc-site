Additional insured

A person or business added to someone else’s liability policy so it is protected for claims arising from that policyholder’s work.

Contracts often require it: a property owner or general contractor asks a subcontractor to name it as an additional insured. The additional insured then has coverage under the subcontractor’s policy for claims tied to that work, within the endorsement’s terms.

A certificate of insurance can show additional insured status, but the endorsement on the policy is what actually grants it.
