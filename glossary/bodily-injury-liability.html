Bodily injury liability

Auto or general liability coverage that pays for injuries you cause to other people, including medical bills, lost wages and legal defense.

On an auto policy it is the first two numbers in a split limit like 100/300/100: $100,000 per person and $300,000 per accident.

It doesn’t pay for your own injuries — that is the job of medical payments, personal injury protection or health insurance.
