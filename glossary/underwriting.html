Underwriting

The process insurers use to decide whether to accept a risk and on what terms and price.

Underwriters look at loss history, property and vehicle details, operations and, for many personal policies, a credit-based insurance score where allowed.

Related terms

  • Loss run
  • Schedule rating
  • Credit-based insurance score

Calculators that use this

  • Loss Run Analyzer — Analyze a multi-year loss run: loss ratio by year, 3- and 5-year loss ratios, frequency and severity.
  • Experience Mod Calculator (NCCI) — Estimate a workers compensation experience modification factor with the NCCI formula: primary/excess split, weighting, ballast and the medical-only reduction.
