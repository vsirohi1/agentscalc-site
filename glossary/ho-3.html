HO-3 (homeowners special form)

The most common homeowners policy, covering the house on an open perils basis and belongings against named perils.

It includes Coverages A to F: dwelling, other structures, personal property, loss of use, personal liability and medical payments to others.

Related terms

  • HO-4 (renters insurance)
  • HO-6 (condo insurance)
  • All-risk (open perils)
