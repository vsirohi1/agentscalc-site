CLUE report

An industry database report of past home and auto insurance claims that insurers check when you apply for coverage.

CLUE (Comprehensive Loss Underwriting Exchange) reports show claims for up to seven years, including the date, type and amount paid. Insurers use them to price and accept policies.

You can request your own report for free once a year and dispute errors.
