Frequency (claim frequency)

How often claims occur, measured as claims per unit of exposure.

A fleet with 12 claims per 100 vehicles has higher frequency than one with 6, even if the total dollars are similar. Frequency is usually driven by safety and operations; severity by limits and claim handling.
