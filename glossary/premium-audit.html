Premium audit

A review after the policy term of a business’s actual payroll or sales, used to adjust a premium based on estimates.

Workers comp and general liability are often written on estimated exposure. If actual exposure was higher, the insured owes additional premium; if lower, it gets a return, subject to any minimum premium.
