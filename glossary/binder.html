Binder

A temporary agreement that puts coverage in force before the full policy is issued.

A binder states who is insured, what coverage applies, the limits and the effective date. It is commonly used when buying a home, so the lender has proof of insurance at closing.

It ends when the policy is issued or on its stated expiration date.
