IBNR (incurred but not reported)

An estimate of losses that have happened but aren’t yet reported, or are reported but not yet fully valued.

Reported losses on recent periods are incomplete. Actuaries use loss development factors to project ultimate losses; the difference from reported losses is IBNR.
