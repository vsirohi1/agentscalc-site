Short-rate cancellation

A cancellation method that returns less than the pro-rata unearned premium, as a penalty for cancelling early.

It applies when the insured cancels on some policies, especially commercial ones. Insurer-initiated cancellations are generally pro-rata.

  • Minimum earned premium
