Comprehensive coverage (other than collision)

Auto coverage for damage to your car from causes other than a crash — theft, fire, hail, flooding, falling objects or hitting an animal.

It pays up to the car’s actual cash value, minus the comprehensive deductible, which is often lower than the collision deductible.

Together with collision it makes up what people call “full coverage.”
