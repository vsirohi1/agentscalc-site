Coinsurance

A property policy condition that reduces a claim payment if the property is insured for less than a set percentage of its value.

With an 80% coinsurance clause on a building worth $1 million, the limit must be at least $800,000. Insure it for $600,000 and a partial loss is paid at 600/800 — 75% of the loss, minus the deductible.

Agreed value or a higher limit avoids the penalty. In health insurance, “coinsurance” means something different: your share of a covered bill.
