Loss trend

The rate at which claim costs change over time from inflation, medical costs and legal factors.

Actuaries trend historical losses forward to the period new rates will cover, often separating frequency and severity trends.

Related terms

  • Loss development factor (LDF)
  • Severity

Calculators that use this

  • Loss Trend Calculator — Trend historical losses to a future policy period with an annual trend rate and exact trend period.
  • Indicated Rate Calculator — Calculate an actuarially indicated rate from pure premium, fixed expenses, variable expenses and profit (pure premium method).
