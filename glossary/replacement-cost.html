Replacement cost

What it costs to repair or replace damaged property with new property of similar kind and quality, without subtracting depreciation.

Replacement cost policies often pay actual cash value first and the rest once you repair or replace. For buildings, replacement cost is the right basis for setting the limit.

Related terms

  • Actual cash value (ACV)
  • Extended replacement cost
  • Insurance to value (ITV)
