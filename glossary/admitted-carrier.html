Admitted carrier

An insurance company licensed by a state’s insurance department to sell insurance there, using rates and forms filed with that state.

Admitted insurers follow the state’s rules on rates and policy forms, and their policyholders are generally protected by the state guaranty fund if the insurer fails.

Risks admitted carriers won’t write can be placed with non-admitted insurers through the surplus lines market.
