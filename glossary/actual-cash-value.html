Actual cash value (ACV)

The value of damaged property just before a loss: what it would cost to replace, minus depreciation.

Actual cash value is replacement cost minus depreciation for age and wear. A seven-year-old roof with a twenty-year life is paid at well below the cost of a new roof on an ACV basis.

Car insurance pays a totaled car’s actual cash value, and many policies pay older roofs or belongings this way unless you buy replacement cost coverage.
