Flood insurance

Separate coverage for flood damage, which home, condo and renters policies exclude.

Most US flood policies come from the National Flood Insurance Program (NFIP), which covers up to $250,000 for a residential building and $100,000 for contents. Private flood insurance can offer higher limits. NFIP policies usually take 30 days to start.
